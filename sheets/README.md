each sheet is one page: abilities and saves, AC/HP/initiative, skills with final bonuses, an attack table, features written out in full, the spell list with slots, equipment, and the character's Toll card, Puck's angle, and the scene they're already tangled in.

## build conventions

- **standard array**, 15 / 14 / 13 / 12 / 10 / 8, before background.
- **2024 rules.** ability bonuses come from the **background** (+2/+1), not the species. every species here predates the 2024 PHB, so their printed Ability Score Increases are **ignored** — that's the standard conversion and it's the only place these builds touch a rules seam.
- every background is 2024 PHB, and each one's origin feat is taken as written: Guide → Magic Initiate (Druid), Entertainer → Musician, Guard → Alert, Charlatan → Skilled.
- fey species are Monsters of the Multiverse except the fairy, which is Wild Beyond the Witchlight. goblinoids and satyrs are creature type **Fey** in MotM, which is why they qualify.
- HP is fixed-average, not rolled.
- all five are legal at 3rd level as written. nobody is holding a feat they can't have yet.

## two table calls to make before session zero

- **Vetchburr's tools.** the Guide background gives cartographer's tools; the Under-Cook post assumes **cook's utensils**. swap them. it costs nothing and the post stops being a lie.
- **Bramwick's armor.** a centaur is a Medium creature, so chain mail works at list price. if your table rules that centaurs need barding, adjust the cost — the AC is unaffected.

## if you want a different five

the pregen doc has ten and two other cuts — "maximum feeling" and "maximum farce". the builds here port cleanly: swap the class and keep the post, the Perquisite, the Toll card, and the collision, which is where the character actually lives.
