# WHINHAW
### the Hedge-Warden · *a tale told by an idiot*

**Fairy · Ranger 3 (Fey Wanderer) · Guide background**
*small fey · speed 30 ft., fly 30 ft. · proficiency bonus +2*

> planted the hawthorn ring around Mullenwake the night the Court first noticed the place, four hundred years ago, and has tended it since the way a gardener tends a garden she considers hers. she does not think this wedding is a good idea. she said so once, to someone who wasn't listening.

## the job

**post:** the Hedge-Warden — you tend the hawthorn ring. you know how the gate works.
**Perquisite (1/session):** open a shortcut through the hedge between any two places in the village.

**your Toll card:** *why she planted the hedge.*
**Puck's angle:** he agrees with her. sincerely, at length, and without asking for anything in return. it is the first time in four centuries anyone has, and it is by a distance the most dangerous thing he does all night.
**where you're already tangled:** the gate itself. you built the thing that freezes solid at moonhigh, and you will be the first to feel it go.

**three things true about you:** you know every gap in the hedge and you put most of them there. you have never once been inside the Court. you kept the village a secret for four hundred years and tonight someone hung lanterns on it.
