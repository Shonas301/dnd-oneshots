# VETCHBURR
### the Under-Cook · *a tale told by an idiot*

**Goblin (Fey) · Druid 3 (Circle of the Land) · Guide background**
*small fey · speed 30 ft. · darkvision 60 ft. · proficiency bonus +2*

> sixty years a food taster in a court that no longer exists, on the reasoning that a goblin is cheap and a duke is not. survived everything anyone put in front of him and developed opinions about all of it. cooks now instead of tasting, and tastes everything anyway, out of habit.

## the job

**post:** the Under-Cook — Peaseblossom's kitchen. you know what's in the punch.
**Perquisite (1/session):** declare that one dish or drink is already in your hands, and say what's in it.

**your Toll card:** *the first meal anyone ever thanked him for.*
**Puck's angle:** he doesn't have to work for it. Vetchburr asks what's in it for him, settles for less, and swaps the salt and the sugar before the conversation is finished. the interesting part is what Puck asks for the *second* time.
**where you're already tangled:** the punch. six gallons, and you are the only creature in the village who knows what is already in it. *Speak with Animals* also settles the honey problem in one conversation, if Cobweb will let you near the hive.

**three things true about you:** you taste everything twice. you have never once been poisoned. you have never once been thanked, and you have stopped waiting.
