### the Seamstress · *a tale told by an idiot*

**Changeling (Fey) · Rogue 3 (Arcane Trickster) · Charlatan background**

> kept a shop in three cities under four faces and got bored of every one of them. took this post because moth-silk is the only thing she has ever met that cannot be lied to. it either hangs or it doesn't.

## features

**Shapechanger.** Bonus Action. change your appearance and voice — any Medium or Small humanoid shape, within reason. **your clothing and equipment do not change with you.** your statistics never change.
**Changeling Instincts.** proficiency in Insight and Persuasion *(included above)*.
**Cunning Action.** Bonus Action: Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
**Steady Aim.** Bonus Action: advantage on your next attack roll this turn, if you haven't moved yet. your Speed becomes 0 until the end of the turn.
**Mage Hand Legerdemain.** your *Mage Hand* is invisible, and it can stow or retrieve an object, pick a lock, or disarm a trap — using your Sleight of Hand check. control it as a Bonus Action.
**Skilled.** the three extra proficiencies above: weaver's tools, disguise kit, Performance.
