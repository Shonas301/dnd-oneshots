# TANSYGLEAM
### the Fiddler · *a tale told by an idiot*

**Satyr · Bard 3 (College of Glamour) · Entertainer background**
*medium fey · speed 35 ft. · proficiency bonus +2*

> played the Summer Court for a century as a favor, then worked out that a favor with no end date is a job. she is very good. she is extremely tired of being very good, and has started making small mistakes on purpose to see whether anyone notices. nobody has.

**Magic Resistance.** advantage on saving throws against spells. *this is enormous. Puck's DC 18 is a coin flip on you.*
**Mirthful Leaps.** add 1d8 feet to any long or high jump, standing or running.
**Bardic Inspiration (d6, 3/long rest).** Bonus Action, one creature within 60 ft. it adds the die to one d20 test within the next hour.
**Expertise.** Performance, Persuasion.
**Beguiling Magic.** *Charm Person* and *Mirror Image* are always prepared. immediately after you cast an Enchantment or Illusion spell with a slot, one creature you can see within 60 ft. makes a DC 13 WIS save or has the Charmed **or** Frightened condition (your choice) for 1 minute. once per Short or Long Rest, or refresh it by expending a spell slot.
**Mantle of Inspiration.** Bonus Action, expend a Bardic Inspiration use and roll it. up to 3 other creatures within 60 ft. each gain temporary HP equal to twice the roll, and each may immediately use its Reaction to move up to its Speed without provoking.
**Musician.** on finishing a Short or Long Rest, play — up to 2 allies who heard you gain Heroic Inspiration.

## the job

**post:** the Fiddler — the processional. everyone must dance.
**Perquisite (1/session):** declare that a piece of music is playing right now, and name it.

**your Toll card:** *the last night she played for free, for nobody, badly.*
**Puck's angle:** he offers the memory back and a room where no one can hear her. she wants to be bad at something again, and he is the only person in four hundred years who has offered.
**where you're already tangled:** the play (§6.2). Dob Cranny wants notes. you have a century of notes and no way to deliver them that isn't cruelty — which is exactly the DC 15 Persuasion to cut his monologue, and exactly the thing you'd have to become to make it *worse* on purpose.

**three things true about you:** you tune by ear and you are never wrong. you have not enjoyed applause since the reign before last. you would like, once, to be asked rather than summoned.
