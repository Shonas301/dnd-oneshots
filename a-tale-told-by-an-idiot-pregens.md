# TEN FOR THE ROSTER
### pregen concepts for *a tale told by an idiot*

ten fae and fae-touched, two per post. level 3, 2024 rules, backstory kept to what fits on the back of a card.

each entry carries three things the one-shot actually needs: a **Toll card** (the memory to surrender at the gate, §4), **Puck's angle** (the lever and the favor, §5), and a **collision** — the Preparation, NPC, or act III beat the character is already tangled in before session zero ends. pick five, hand out the matching Perquisite, and every scene has someone in the room who cares.

**built sheets:** five of these are statted out at level 3 in [`sheets/`](sheets/) — Vetchburr, Tansygleam, Bramwick, Quillflit, Whinhaw. one page each, print and hand out.

**note on Fey Touched:** in the 2024 PHB it's a general feat, level 4+, not an origin feat. at level 3 a mortal gets fae-touched through species, a pact, or a Magic Initiate origin feat off a background. everything below is legal as written at 3rd.

## the under-cook
*Peaseblossom's kitchen. Perquisite: declare a dish is already in your hands, and say what's in it.*

### 1. Vetchburr
**Goblin (Fey) · Druid (Circle of the Land) · Guide background (Magic Initiate: Druid)**

sixty years a food taster in a court that no longer exists, on the reasoning that a goblin is cheap and a duke is not. survived everything anyone put in front of him and developed opinions about all of it. cooks now instead of tasting, and tastes everything anyway, out of habit.

- **toll:** the first meal anyone ever thanked him for.
- **puck's angle:** doesn't need much. asks what's in it for him, settles for less, does the salt-and-sugar swap before the conversation is over. the interesting part is what he asks for on the *second* favor.
- **collision:** the punch (§6.1). six gallons, and he is the only person in the village who knows what's already in it.

### 2. Mattie Ash
**Hexblood · Cleric (Trickery Domain) · Hermit background (Healer)**

her grandmother made a bargain over a cradle and paid in Mattie's hair, which grew back black and moving. she serves a small god the village never named — a pantry god, about bread, about leaving a light on. this is the largest kitchen she has ever stood in and she is not nervous, which is upsetting everyone else.

- **toll:** her grandmother's face. she remembers the bargain perfectly and not the woman at all.
- **puck's angle:** he shows up wearing the grandmother. that's the entire pitch; he doesn't even name a favor the first time.
- **collision:** the last word of a dying man (§6.1). she's the one person at the table with a professional opinion about how to get one, and Puck's obvious solution will be aimed at her.

## the fiddler
*the processional. Perquisite: declare what music is playing right now, and name it.*

### 3. Tansygleam
**Satyr · Bard (College of Glamour) · Entertainer background (Musician)**

played the Summer Court for a century as a favor, then worked out that a favor with no end date is a job. she is very good. she is extremely tired of being very good, and has started making small mistakes on purpose to see whether anyone notices. nobody has.

- **toll:** the last night she played for free, for nobody, badly.
- **puck's angle:** he offers the memory back plus a room where no one can hear her. she wants to be bad at something again and he is the only one who has ever offered.
- **collision:** the play (§6.2). Dob Cranny wants notes. she has a century of notes and no way to give them that isn't cruelty.

### 4. Hob Quickset
**Harengon · Bard (College of Dance) · Wayfarer background (Lucky)**

a fae road opened under his feet at fourteen and let him out somewhere with better weather. he hasn't stopped moving since. plays standing on furniture. has never once finished a conversation, including this one.

- **toll:** where he was going.
- **puck's angle:** Puck knows the road. offers directions. wants the west gate unlatched for six minutes at moonrise, which is nothing, which is the point.
- **collision:** act III (§8). he's the one who left the gate open, or he's the one who didn't, and either way he'll be doing the arithmetic while the Bride speaks line one.

## the doorwarden
*the list, the coats, the throwing-out. Perquisite: declare one creature is on, or not on, the list.*

### 5. Bramwick of the Nine Orchards
**Centaur · Paladin (Oath of the Ancients) · Guard background (Alert)**

grew up in the orchards. took the oath at nineteen because a woman he admired had taken one, and has now kept it a great deal longer than she did. he does not know what became of her. he believes a guest list is a moral document and will say so to anyone.

- **toll:** the sound of her voice.
- **puck's angle:** Puck doesn't ask for sabotage. he offers a name, for free, and lets it sit there all night.
- **collision:** **the Bride** (§8). the woman is Sela of the Nine Orchards. if you use this concept, the DC 15 History check is already answered and the DC 18 Persuasion is aimed straight at a PC's chest — the fight can end in a sentence, spoken kindly, by the one person entitled to speak it. worth the whole build. don't tell the player which orchard she left from.

### 6. Ollamy Grieve
**Bugbear (Fey) · Fighter (Battle Master) · Soldier background (Savage Attacker)**

seven feet of impeccable manners. was a nightmare once — the professional kind, contracted, paid by the season — and quit on discovering he preferred people awake. runs a door like a maître d' and has never had to hit anyone twice.

- **toll:** the last thing he was ever frightened of.
- **puck's angle:** flattery about the old work, delivered like a man reminiscing with a colleague. the favor: put back on the list one name Thistlewick struck off — an old Court scandal, invited home.
- **collision:** the play (§6.2). that guest arriving is exactly the scandal the mortals can accidentally reenact on stage. two Verses, and Thistlewick will know precisely who let it happen.

## the seamstress
*Titania's train, Oberon's collar, sixty yards of moth-silk. Perquisite: declare you have already altered a garment someone is wearing.*

### 7. Quillflit
**Changeling (Fey) · Rogue (Arcane Trickster) · Charlatan background (Skilled)**

kept a shop in three cities under four faces and got bored of every one of them. took this post because moth-silk is the only thing she has ever met that cannot be lied to. it either hangs or it doesn't.

- **toll:** her own face — the one underneath. she cannot get back to it tonight and is pretending that's fine.
- **puck's angle:** he's wearing it. he doesn't mention it and he doesn't take it off.
- **collision:** everything. a changeling on staff makes the whole village a set of doors, and it makes Puck's *Shifting Shape* a genuine problem rather than a party trick.

### 8. Pippin Underhaw
**Gnome (Forest) · Wizard (Illusionist) · Artisan background (Crafter)**

apprenticed to a mortal tailor, then to a fae one. the second apprenticeship is not over and is not going to be. holds that illusion is tailoring with fewer pins. wants, more than he'd admit under oath, for Titania to notice a hem.

- **toll:** the last thing he made to his own design.
- **puck's angle:** a commission. real, court-witnessed, his name on it. the favor is one seam left unfinished on Titania's train, and Puck is very clear that nobody will ever see it.
- **collision:** the Crown (§6.3). Moth and Mustardseed have been arguing for six hours and he is constitutionally incapable of leaving unfinished work in a room with him.

## the hedge-warden
*the hawthorn ring and how the gate works. Perquisite: open a shortcut through the hedge between any two places in the village.*

### 9. Sorrel Vance
**Eladrin · Warlock (Archfey) · Sage background (Magic Initiate: Wizard)**

signed in a hedge behind his grandmother's house at eleven and came back the wrong shape, in a wrong season, and has been running about a season behind ever since. his patron is at this wedding. he has never seen them and does not know which one they are. his grandmother still leaves a plate out.

- **toll:** the taste of her pears.
- **puck's angle:** Puck knows which one the patron is and will say so. the favor: steal the pear from Old Mother Vance's tree.
- **collision:** *a pear stolen from a mortal who deserved it* (§6.1). she does not deserve it, the pear turns bitter, and the whole table tastes it — and he is her grandson. the Ruin condition and the family reunion are the same scene.

### 10. Whinhaw
**Fairy · Ranger (Fey Wanderer) · Guide background (Magic Initiate: Druid)**

planted the hawthorn ring around Mullenwake the night the Court first noticed the place, four hundred years ago, and has tended it since the way a gardener tends a garden she considers hers. she does not think this wedding is a good idea. she said so once, to someone who wasn't listening.

- **toll:** why she planted the hedge.
- **puck's angle:** he agrees with her. sincerely, at length, and without asking for anything. it's the first time in four centuries anyone has, and it is by a distance the most dangerous thing he does all night.
- **collision:** the gate itself (§4, §8). she built the thing that freezes solid in act III, and she'll be the first to feel it go.

## cutting to five

one per post, and aim for the 2 / 2 / 1 split §5 wants.

- **maximum cross-purpose:** Vetchburr, Tansygleam, Bramwick, Quillflit, Whinhaw. two who want it perfect, two who want it burned, one who hasn't decided and won't.
- **maximum feeling:** Mattie Ash, Tansygleam, Bramwick, Pippin Underhaw, Sorrel Vance. every Toll card in this set is about a person, and act IV will cost the table something.
- **maximum farce:** Vetchburr, Hob Quickset, Ollamy Grieve, Quillflit, Sorrel Vance. nobody here is going to say no to Puck and three of them will say yes twice.

if you take Bramwick, take him seriously — §8 has a different shape when a PC arrives already holding the key to it.
